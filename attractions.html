MAX: 56 days ahead (8 wk · 3pm HI time · opens ≈ Jun 22-23) · MIN: ~1-2 days ahead · recreation.gov · $1 · sells out in minutes
💎 Diamond Head
MAX: 30 days ahead (opens ≈ Jul 18-19) · MIN: same day if slots remain · gostateparks.hawaii.gov · $5 pp + $10 parking · last entry 4pm

Alternative: Explorer Pass — choose 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7 specific attractions (not the whole catalog); valid 60 days from first use; you pick which ones as you go, no need to decide at purchase

★ 4.0 (328) · from MX$3,919 per person, price at the lowest tier (2 days); rises with days chosen
